Lore

Hero Ahead of Time

Metroman, voiced by Brad Pitt, is the hero of Megamind and Megamind 2, and remains one of DreamWorks’ most iconic characters. Released in November 2010, the original film grossed over $361 million worldwide, leaving a lasting mark on Gen Z’s collective childhood. Metroman’s design and heroic flair were far ahead of their time, setting a new standard for animated superheroes in the decade that followed..

So Bad It's Good

Fourteen years later, Megamind 2 (2024) stumbled into infamy with a 6% Rotten Tomatoes score — the lowest in DreamWorks history — reviving the series not through glory, but through meme culture. The movie was so bad it transcended criticism, becoming an ironic masterpiece of internet humor, where fans celebrated its absurdity more than its plot, transforming it from a cinematic failure into a digital cult phenomenon.

Flop to Fame - English Virality

What started as a joke became a movement. The Metroman dance, originally just a few seconds in the movie, caught fire in English meme culture. Edits, remixes, and meme compilations flooded YouTube and TikTok, each one exaggerating his divine rhythm and unshakable confidence. Before long, it spiraled into a real-life dancing cult, with fans recreating the moves at parties and conventions. Today, Videos of the Metroman dance universe has racked up over 10 million views and counting, making him not just a meme, but a SuperMeme.

Chinese Adoption

In October, a video of of Metroman's "splitting the bus" voiceover exploded across Chinese social media, spawning a flood of hilarious province (state) themed voiceovers that only the locals truly get. The trend has gone viral nationwide, with countless edits pulling in millions of views and even inspiring full on cosplay recreations. Right now, Metroman is at the peak of his meme power, taking over countries.
